Signal Radar Vision

Mission

Build a personal investment radar that automatically discovers high-value market signals before they become widely known.

The system combines independent public sources and transforms them into a small number of explainable opportunities requiring human review.

The goal is not to predict the market.

The goal is to reduce the amount of information a human investor must inspect.

⸻

Success

Signal Radar is successful when:

* Every day it produces only a small number of opportunities.
* Every opportunity can be explained.
* Every opportunity can be audited back to the original public sources.
* Running the pipeline twice produces the same result.
* False positives are progressively reduced.
* The system remains simple.

⸻

Product Principles

Always prefer:

* simplicity
* explainability
* reproducibility
* deterministic behaviour
* maintainability
* real public data

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

⸻

Long-term Vision

The radar evolves through layers.

Layer 1
Data collection.

Layer 2
Signal normalization.

Layer 3
Cluster detection.

Layer 4
Correlation.

Layer 5
Prioritisation.

Layer 6
Alert delivery.

Layer 7
Performance measurement.

Layer 8
Continuous improvement.

⸻

Engineering Philosophy

Every new feature must make the radar more useful for a human investor.

Never add complexity without increasing product value.

When multiple implementations are possible, choose the simplest one that preserves explainability.
